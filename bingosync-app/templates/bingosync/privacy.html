We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking, and we don't sell or share your data. Fonts and all other files are served from this site, so your browser doesn't contact anyone else.

What others can see

Your username, the date you joined and your stats and achievements are shown on your public profile. In a room, everyone in it sees your name, color, the goals you mark and your chat messages, and finished rooms are listed in Room History.

Cookies

We only use cookies the site needs to work: a session cookie that keeps you logged in and remembers which rooms you've joined (expires after two weeks), and a security cookie that protects forms against cross-site request forgery. Because these are strictly necessary, we don't ask for consent to them.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and game data: until you delete your account.
  • Server logs: rotated automatically as they fill and overwritten, usually within days.
  • Rate-limiting records: an hour at most.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or restrict the use of your data, object to its use, or get a copy of it to take elsewhere. Two of these you can do yourself, from your profile page while logged in:

  • Download your data as a file.
  • Delete your account. This removes your account, email, stats and chat messages, and removes your name from past rooms. Goals you marked stay on old boards but are no longer linked to you.

For anything else, use the contact above. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, such as the one where you live.
