Do you need an account?

No. You can create rooms and play, count or spectate in them without one. An account adds the following:

With an account

  • Your wins, losses, bingos, squares marked and games played are tracked
  • Achievements and a public profile page
  • An OBS link for your room's board, to use as a stream overlay
  • Your username is reserved as your name in every room
  • Rejoin a room as the same player, with your color and marks

Without an account

  • Create rooms and join as Player, Counter or Spectator
  • Pick any display name each time
  • No stats or achievements are recorded
  • No OBS link
